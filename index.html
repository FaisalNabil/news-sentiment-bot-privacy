Privacy Policy

News Sentiment Bot & Info-Graph-IQ
Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Facebook/Meta application News Sentiment Bot (“the App”), operated in connection with the Facebook Page Info-Graph-IQ, handles information.

1. Who we are

The App is a Page management tool used to publish educational map and data posts to the Info-Graph-IQ Facebook Page. Contact: nabilt59@gmail.com.

2. What the App does

The App uses Meta’s Graph API with a Page access token to:

  • Create and publish posts (text, images, and related media) on Pages we administer
  • Read Page engagement and insights so we can improve content
  • List Pages associated with the authenticated admin account

3. Information we access

Depending on granted permissions, the App may access:

  • Public Page content we publish (posts, photos, captions)
  • Page-level engagement metrics and insights (e.g. reactions, reach)
  • Page identity and admin-linked Page list needed to publish
  • Basic public profile fields required by Meta Login for Business, if used

The App is intended to manage our own Page(s). It is not designed to collect personal data from general Facebook users for advertising profiles or resale.

4. How we use information

  • To publish scheduled or automated educational content on Info-Graph-IQ
  • To monitor post performance and fix publishing issues
  • To maintain secure API authentication (access tokens stored server-side)

5. Data storage and retention

Page access tokens and operational logs are stored on systems we control and are retained only as long as needed to operate the App and comply with Meta platform requirements. We do not sell personal data.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data may be processed by Meta (Facebook) as part of normal Graph API / Page publishing, and by hosting providers that run our bot infrastructure, solely to operate the App.

7. Third-party services

Publishing and analytics occur through Meta Platforms, Inc. Their terms and policies also apply when you interact with Facebook products: Meta Privacy Policy.

8. Your choices

Page admins can revoke the App’s access at any time from Facebook Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites. For deletion or other requests related to data we store, email nabilt59@gmail.com.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: nabilt59@gmail.com
